# CampusQuestAR

# Overview
A mixed-reality game called Campus Quest AR uses Unity to turn a university campus into an interactive quest setting. Players use their mobile devices to explore real-world landmarks where digital objects and puzzles appear using AR Foundation and geolocation. Mini-games with a physics theme, 3D object interactions, and campus-related trivia are among the challenges. Level progression leads to the final "Campus Gate" being unlocked. The design permits future additions like adaptive difficulty and procedural quest generation, despite its primary focus on Unity engineering.

# Gameplay
In Campus Quest AR, the player spends most of their time exploring real-world locations on campus while interacting with digital overlays through their mobile device. From the map screen, they identify quest markers anchored to campus landmarks and physically navigate to those spots. Once there, the AR mode activates, revealing interactive objects such as treasure chests, glowing portals, or NPC guardians.
The core loop revolves around solving challenges at each landmark. These range from physics-based puzzles, such as balancing virtual objects on platforms, to interactive mini-games like assembling 3D models or aligning symbols. Trivia and riddle challenges tied to the campus also appear, requiring the player to think quickly before moving on. Success at each location rewards players with digital keys, badges, or progress tokens that move them closer to unlocking the final objective.
Gameplay can be compared to scavenger hunt-style AR experiences like Pokémon Go, but instead of catching creatures, the player is actively solving puzzles and unlocking progression-based challenges. The emphasis is less on collection and more on interaction, problem-solving, and exploration. This keeps players engaged not only through movement across campus but also through varied AR-based puzzle-solving mechanics.
